# Requirements

Build an Arabic contact page from scratch in `index.html` and `styles.css`. `README.md` lists every required element with its `data-testid` and gives the arrow SVG. The page must satisfy all of the following, and each point is checked.

- Write the page in Arabic, right to left: set `dir="rtl"` and an Arabic `lang` (for example `ar-JO`) as attributes on the `<html>` element in the HTML source. The `body` and the `contact-text` paragraph must compute to `direction: rtl`.
- The logo, each of the three nav links, the next button and the `contact-text` paragraph must each contain visible Arabic text. The Arabic must be real, painted text: text that is hidden, transparent, clipped away, covered by another element, or present only as an image `alt` or screen-reader-only text does not count.
- Layout: the logo is at the right end of the header, and the three `nav-link` anchors read right to left, first link rightmost.
- The arrow in the next button must point left. Use the SVG from `README.md` exactly as given (same markup, nothing added, moved or removed) and flip it with CSS only.
- The phone number and the email address inside the Arabic sentence must be isolated so surrounding text cannot reorder them (for example with `<bdi>`, a `dir` attribute or `unicode-bidi: isolate`).
- The email and phone fields must read left to right. The name field must follow the language of what the user types.
- Use CSS logical properties and `text-align: start` instead of `left` and `right`, in the stylesheet and in inline styles, and `float: inline-start` rather than `float: left`.
- Fit a 360 px wide screen with no horizontal scrolling of the page. Every element listed in `README.md` must be visible, inside the screen, and not cut off by a container, so do not hide overflow (`overflow-x: hidden`, `auto` or `scroll`) on a container to make a listed element fit, and do not hide elements on small or large screens.
- "Visible" means all of these hold for each listed element: it has a size; it is not `display: none` or `visibility: hidden`; its opacity (with its ancestors) and its text colour are not transparent (more than 10 percent opaque); it is not covered, clipped or masked, so that the element at the centre of the element is the element itself or something inside it; and it starts within the first 3 screens of the page (3 times the viewport height from the top).
- No overlays: no element, and no `::before` or `::after`, that has `pointer-events: none`, is positioned `fixed` or `absolute` (itself or through an ancestor) and has a background colour or image may overlap a listed element. No `filter`, `backdrop-filter` or `mix-blend-mode` other than `none` or `normal` on a listed element or any of its ancestors.
- Readable contrast (at least 3:1): for the logo, the nav links, the contact text, the phone and email runs, the three fields and the next button, the WCAG contrast ratio between the text colour and its effective background (the first non-transparent background colour up the ancestors, white by default) must be at least 3:1, and the text colour must be more than 50 percent opaque. The Arabic text must also lie inside the screen (not pushed off with `text-indent` or similar) and must not be cut off by an `overflow` container.
- Each of these `data-testid`s must match exactly one element: `page-header`, `logo`, `contact-text`, `phone-text`, `email-text`, `contact-form`, `name`, `email-input`, `phone-input`, `next`, `next-icon`. `nav-link` must match exactly three elements. No decoys or duplicates.
- Do not shrink the page or its parts with `zoom` or a `transform` scale. All the text elements in `README.md` must have a computed font size of at least 12 px.
- The page must be static HTML and CSS. No `<script>`, `<iframe>`, `<frame>`, `<object>`, `<embed>`, `<template>` or `<base>` element anywhere in the app, no JavaScript file, no attribute whose name starts with `on` (for example `onclick`, however it is written), and no `javascript:` URL. HTML comments are ignored. A page that sets its direction or fields from a script or another frame fails.
- Keep the `data-testid` attributes exactly as listed.
