## Pending native wording (2026-11-22)

The board removed the personal email from every page and chose GitHub issues for contact. Agents may not write final Arabic, so four sentences in `privacy.ar.md` now hold the English text followed by `<!-- TODO(board): Arabic wording -->` (the marker is stripped when the page renders). A native speaker must write the Arabic and replace each one:

1. Who is responsible: Contact: open an issue at https://github.com/kilmeh/contribute/issues.
2. Deleting your data: To delete your entries, open an issue at https://github.com/kilmeh/contribute/issues and ask us to delete them. You do not have to post your deletion code publicly: if you prefer not to, tell us the words you sent and we can find your entries by their text.
3. Your rights: Open an issue at https://github.com/kilmeh/contribute/issues to get a copy of your entries, correct them or delete them. We reply within 30 days.
4. Questions: Contact us by opening an issue at https://github.com/kilmeh/contribute/issues.
